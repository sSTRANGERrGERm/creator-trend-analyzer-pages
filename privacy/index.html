Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Creator Trend Analyzer handles information when you use the tool to connect your own TikTok account and analyze creator performance data.

Information We Access

If you connect TikTok, Creator Trend Analyzer may access information permitted by the scopes you approve, such as basic profile information and public video information. This may include video identifiers, captions or descriptions, creation times, duration, view counts, like counts, comment counts, and share counts where available through TikTok's official APIs.

How Information Is Used

The information is used to show analytics, calculate engagement metrics, compare video performance over time, and provide content improvement insights such as trend fit, caption patterns, posting behavior, and engagement signals.

Local Storage

Creator Trend Analyzer is designed as a local desktop tool. Retrieved metrics and snapshots are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly choose to export, share, or back up the data elsewhere.

Sharing and Selling Data

We do not sell your personal information. The tool does not share your TikTok data with third parties except as necessary to communicate with TikTok APIs that you authorize.

Account Connection and Removal

You can disconnect the app from your TikTok account through TikTok's app permissions or by deleting the local tokens and stored data from your device.

Security

The tool stores data locally and uses OAuth-based authorization for TikTok access. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so you should protect your device and account credentials.

Children

Creator Trend Analyzer is intended for creators and businesses who are old enough to manage social media accounts under applicable platform rules. It is not directed to children.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
